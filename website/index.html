01 THE IDEA

Most voice tools lock you to one provider and one workflow. Grain treats the model as a detail — and the flow as the product.

02 ARCHITECTURE

Two layers.
Both yours to swap.

Grain separates speech recognition from text processing, and exposes both through OpenAI-compatible endpoints. Bring a hosted API, a self-hosted server, or an on-device model — the workflow never changes.

L1 OPENAI-COMPATIBLE

Transcription

Your speech becomes text through whichever STT backend you choose — cloud for accuracy today, on-device when you need it private.

  • Cloud STT — Deepgram, AssemblyAI, Groq
  • On-device — Local Parakeet, no audio leaves the machine
  • Smart rotation across enabled providers
ROUTE
L2 OPENAI-COMPATIBLE

Processing

Hand the transcript to any LLM with your own directive prompt — clean it up, rewrite it, or turn it into an action. Or skip it entirely and paste raw.

  • Any LLM — Gemini, Groq, Cerebras, OpenAI
  • Custom directive prompts per task
  • Vocabulary dictionary for tricky terms

03 THE WIDGET

A pill that
gets out of the way.

Trigger your hotkey and a small pill appears at your cursor. The left side pulses with your live voice level so you know it's listening. Speak, release, and your text is ready — no window to switch to, no focus stolen.
